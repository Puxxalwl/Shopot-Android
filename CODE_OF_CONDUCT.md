# Contributor Covenant

**English** | [Русский](ru/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to our community:
- Being respectful of differing opinions
- Welcoming newcomers
- Accepting constructive feedback

## Reporting

Report abuse through GitHub's private *Security → Report a vulnerability*
tab, or a private message to a maintainer's GitHub profile.
